were returned; unrelated Go/Python matches were rejected. Pinned primary source
and Apple documentation supplied the fallback. RustCrypto AEAD documentation
was resolved and queried through Context7; `srp`'s primary docs disclose no
independent third-party audit. Source URLs/versions are in the ADR.

Hardware actually tested: **none**. No server/mDNS advertisement was started.
No owner-assisted iPhone/iPad trial or independent HAP controller was available
in this session. Discovery, encrypted pairing/session protocol, HTTP reads/writes,
subscriptions, process restart with real controller, topology refresh, protocol
revocation and clean HAP shutdown are **not verified**. Storage-level tests do
not substitute for any of these. No complete advisory audit, current MFi-spec
access, certification or human HAP/security sign-off is claimed.

No pairing keys, PIN, gateway identifiers or household data were committed.
Private state and upstream/vendor/build directories remain ignored. Existing
`.serena/` is preserved and unstaged. Homebridge, production pairings, rooms,
scenes, automations and equipment were untouched.
